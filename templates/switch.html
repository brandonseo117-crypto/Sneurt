How to Play

1. Placement Phase

Look at the target image and place it onto the board where you think it fits along the activation gradient.

2. Sorting Phase

Click or drag unlocked tiles to swap their positions into the correct sequence.

3. Tile Indicators

  • Green: Tile is in the correct spot and locked!
  • Yellow: Tile is exactly 1 spot away from its spot.
  • Red: Tile is in the wrong position.

4. Direction & Assistance

The game automatically locks sequence direction (Low → High or High → Low) based on your first evaluations. Struggling? Synaptic Assist will step in to help!
